# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in PageKit to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with other people
- Respecting different opinions and experience
- Giving and gracefully accepting useful feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of behaviour that isn't acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, such as issues and pull requests, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [Frank Thamel](https://github.com/frankthamel). Every report will be reviewed fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may ban anyone from the project, temporarily or for good.
